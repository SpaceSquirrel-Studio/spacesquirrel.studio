Tactile controls for anything in Blender, right on the model, where your eyes already are.

Hover a part

Controls appear on the surface right under your mouse. No more digging through an entire UI.

Click and drag

Each drag direction can do something different. Hold Alt mid-drag to fluidly switch, and the whole move undoes in one step. Click to open deeper menus and custom handles.

Drive anything

Bones, modifier inputs, any property. A posing tool for animators, a configurator for designers.

It teaches itself.

Build or use step-by-step tutorials that play right inside Blender, each with short clips and an interactive practice scene.

Learn by doing

Each step points to the control you need, ticks itself off when you've done it, and moves to the next step.

Write your own

Build tutorials for your own rigs, tools and workflows. They travel with the file, so your users learn your controls the moment they open it.

Help that gets out of your way

Choose Learn, Inform or Quiet. Learn when you need it, work in peace when you don't.

Free does it all. Pro does it faster.

Kneady Controls is free and complete: build controls on any model, and play everything anyone builds with Kneady, including in Pro. Addon creators can drive their own tools with Kneady, and their users won't need to buy a thing.

Pro is for the people who build controls again and again, like riggers, studios and character sellers. Templates, mirroring and shareable region maps trim hours off setup.
