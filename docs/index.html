Tutorials in Kneady

These tutorials play inside Blender. When Help is set to Learn (the default), they are listed under Start a Tutorial at the top of the Kneady tab. Each one plays in a panel next to the view, in its own practice scene, and checks off each step as you complete it. The guide uses their clips as examples. See Tutorials in the guide.

  1. 0Learn, Inform and QuietChoose how much Kneady tells you: Learn's tutorials and complete tips, Inform's shorter tips, or Quiet.
  2. 1Quick StartPut a region on the default cube, give it faces, and drag its button to turn the cube.
  3. 2Change the Default ControlsGive a button your own commands: slide the cube left and right, grow it up and down, at the speed you choose.
  4. 3Drive More Than BonesMake a button drive a shape key and a modifier input, two things at once, and switch between its two drags with Alt.
  5. 4Build a MenuGive a button a menu, add buttons to it, name them, and choose how they look.
  6. 5Menus in UseDrag a menu's buttons, pin a menu open, make one stay open, and put a menu inside a menu.
  7. 6Toggles and Show WhenMake a toggle that switches an arm between IK and FK, and buttons that show only in the mode they work in.
  8. 7The Look of Your ButtonsLine buttons up, show their names, make them text or square, and outline a region instead of filling it.
  9. 8Buttons on the ItemPut a button on the item itself, drag it where it goes, watch it ride the pose, and see it from behind.
  10. 93D HandlesTurn a button on the item into a 3D handle: an arrow each way it moves, a ring for a turn, and a drag along it.
  11. 10Viewport EditEdit buttons where you see them: pick one on the item, add one, rearrange the row, and remove a menu you don't need.
  12. 11Copy and ReuseCopy one button's commands onto another, tuck a button into a menu, and give a region another region's buttons.
  13. 12Tips for Your ButtonsRead a button's tip, say what its drag does in your own words, add a message, and choose how much Kneady says.
  14. 13Write a TutorialWrite a tutorial of your own in the Kneady tab: a step, what ticks it off, and Try to play it.

Writing a tutorial

For add-on developers, riggers and character sellers. You can write tutorials in the Kneady tab, include them with an add-on, or attach them to a rig so they are saved in its file.

The tutorials' clips and practice scenes are public domain (CC0 1.0): use them for anything. Questions go to the Space Squirrel Discord.
